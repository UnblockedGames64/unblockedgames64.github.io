Moto X3M: How to Earn 3 Stars on Every Level

By NitroRider | February 26, 2026 | Category: Strategy Guides

Moto X3M is the ultimate side-scrolling bike racer. It combines fast-paced action with deadly obstacles and physics-based puzzles. To get the coveted 3-star rating on every level, you can't just drive fast—you have to drive smart. Time is your only enemy, and every second counts.

1. The Power of the Flip

In Moto X3M, stunts aren't just for show. Every front flip or back flip you perform subtracts 0.5 seconds from your final time. This is the secret to beating the strict 3-star time limits. Whenever you are in the air for a significant amount of time, try to squeeze in as many flips as possible. Just make sure you land on your wheels, or the time save won't matter!

2. Throttle Control

It is tempting to hold down the "W" or Up Arrow key for the entire race, but that will often lead to a fiery explosion. Many of the traps in Moto X3M are designed to catch riders who are going at full speed. Learn to "feather" the throttle—tap it in short bursts to maintain control over bumpy terrain or when approaching a precarious ledge.

3. Landing the Jump

How you land is just as important as how you take off. When coming off a jump, try to tilt your bike so that both wheels hit the ground at the same time, or slightly rear-wheel first. If you land on your front wheel, your bike will lose momentum or, worse, flip you over. Keeping your bike parallel to the slope of the landing zone ensures you maintain maximum speed.

4. Don't Fear the Restart

Moto X3M is a game of trial and error. You will blow up—a lot. Don't be discouraged. Each failure teaches you where a hidden trap is located or exactly how much speed you need to clear a gap. Use the checkpoints wisely, but remember that for a perfect 3-star run, you usually need a "clean" run with zero deaths.
