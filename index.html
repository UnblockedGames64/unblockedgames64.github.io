Quick Strategy Guides

🏃 Mastering Endless Runners

Games like Slope and Run 3 require fast reflexes. Here's how to survive longer:

  • Look ahead: Don't look at your character; keep your eyes focused on the upcoming obstacles.
  • Stay centered: Keep your avatar in the middle of the track whenever possible to maximize your dodge options.

🧩 Puzzle Game Tips

Stuck on 2048 or Block Blast? Keep these strategies in mind:

  • Pick a corner: In 2048, always keep your highest tile locked into one specific corner.
  • Take your time: Most puzzle games don't have timers. Think two steps ahead before you click!
