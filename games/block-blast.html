🎮 How to Play Tetris

The controls are straightforward, but success requires planning and precision:

  • Arrow Keys: Move the tetromino left or right.
  • Up Arrow: Rotate the tetromino to fit it into gaps.
  • Down Arrow: Soft drop the piece faster to the bottom.
  • Spacebar: Hard drop the piece instantly.
  • Objective: Clear as many lines as possible without letting the stack reach the top of the screen.

As pieces fall, it’s important to think ahead. Successful players plan for upcoming tetrominoes, aiming to keep the stack even and create opportunities for clearing multiple lines at once.

🎯 Tips for Beginners

If you’re new to Tetris, follow these beginner-friendly strategies:

  • Keep your stack flat — uneven stacks make future placements difficult.
  • Focus on clearing lines instead of just placing pieces quickly.
  • Save the long “I” tetrominoes for clearing multiple lines at once.
  • Rotate pieces strategically rather than rotating randomly.
  • Stay calm as speed increases — panic leads to mistakes.

🔥 Advanced Strategies

For experienced players, Tetris offers opportunities for advanced techniques to maximize score and efficiency:

  • T-Spin: Rotate T-shaped pieces into tight spaces for bonus points.
  • Back-to-Back Clears: Chain line clears consecutively for higher scores.
  • Stack Management: Keep one column slightly lower for “I” tetromino placement.
  • Speed Control: Adjust drop speed carefully to maintain accuracy under pressure.

Mastering these techniques requires planning and foresight, which is why Tetris continues to be a challenging and rewarding puzzle game even decades after its release.
