🎮 How to Play Snake

Snake is easy to pick up but challenging to master. The controls and objectives are straightforward:

  • Arrow Keys / WASD: Move the snake up, down, left, or right.
  • Objective: Collect food to grow your snake while avoiding collisions with walls or yourself.
  • Score Points: Each food item collected increases your score and the snake’s length.
  • Survival: The longer you survive and the more food you collect, the higher your score.

Planning your movements and anticipating the snake’s growth are key. One wrong turn can end your run, but every success is satisfying and encourages you to try again.

🎯 Tips for Beginners

If you’re new to Snake, these tips will help you get started:

  • Start slowly and plan your moves to avoid crashing.
  • Focus on keeping the snake in open areas to give yourself room to maneuver.
  • Use the edges strategically, but avoid corner traps where you might get stuck.
  • Prioritize survival over collecting every piece of food immediately.
  • Practice controlling the snake’s speed and direction for smoother gameplay.

🔥 Advanced Strategies

As you become more skilled, advanced strategies can help you achieve higher scores:

  • Plan your path in advance, leaving space for the snake to grow.
  • Create a looping pattern to control movement and avoid accidental collisions.
  • Use the snake’s body as a guide to manage tight spaces.
  • Stay calm as the speed increases — precision beats speed in the long run.
  • Learn to anticipate how the snake will move after collecting food to avoid getting trapped.
