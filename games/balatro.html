🎮 How to Play

The basics of Balatro are simple enough: play poker hands to score points and beat the current “blind” (a round with a target score). As the game progresses, the blinds get harder and require more points to pass. Along the way, you’ll collect Jokers, booster packs, and deck upgrades that completely reshape your strategy.

  • Play poker hands: Pairs, three-of-a-kind, full houses, flushes — all the usual hands apply, but you’re scoring points, not chips.
  • Jokers change the rules: Each Joker card has a special ability, like doubling your multipliers, giving bonus points for certain suits, or even breaking poker rules entirely.
  • Booster packs: These give you new cards, upgrades, and tools to expand your deck’s potential.
  • Survive the blinds: Each round has a minimum score requirement. Fail to meet it, and your run ends.
