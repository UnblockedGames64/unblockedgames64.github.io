🎮 How to Play Tetris

The rules of Tetris are straightforward, making it accessible to players of all ages:

  • Arrow Keys: Move tetrominoes left or right to fit them into place.
  • Up Arrow: Rotate the tetromino to fit it correctly.
  • Down Arrow: Soft drop the piece faster for quicker placement.
  • Spacebar: Hard drop the tetromino instantly to the bottom.
  • Goal: Complete horizontal lines to clear them and prevent the stack from reaching the top.

The key to success is planning ahead — think about where upcoming tetrominoes will fit and try to create complete lines with minimal gaps. Clearing multiple lines at once earns more points and creates space for longer survival.

🎯 Tips for Beginners

If you’re new to Tetris, here are some strategies to help you get started:

  • Keep the stack as flat as possible — avoid creating tall peaks that are hard to fill.
  • Focus on creating full lines rather than just placing pieces randomly.
  • Use rotations wisely to fit pieces into tight spaces.
  • Don’t panic as the speed increases — stay calm and plan your moves quickly.
  • Save long pieces (the I-shaped tetromino) for clearing multiple lines at once.

🔥 Advanced Strategies

Once you’re comfortable with the basics, you can try more advanced techniques to maximize your score and survive longer:

  • T-Spin: Rotate T-shaped pieces into tight spots for bonus points.
  • Back-to-Back Clears: Clear multiple line combos consecutively for higher scores.
  • Stack Management: Keep one column slightly lower to prepare for I-shaped pieces.
  • Speed Control: Adjust your drop speed to balance speed and accuracy.

Advanced players focus on efficiency and foresight, predicting upcoming tetrominoes and planning placements several moves ahead. This skill is what separates casual players from Tetris masters.
