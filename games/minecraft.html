💡 Tips for Beginners

If you’re new to Minecraft, the first day in the world can be intimidating. Here are a few essential tips to get started:

  • Start by punching trees to collect wood — it’s the foundation for all crafting.
  • Craft a crafting table and wooden tools right away to speed up progress.
  • Find coal or craft charcoal for torches before nightfall to keep monsters away.
  • Build a simple dirt or wooden hut to protect yourself until you’re ready to explore.
  • Always bring food — running out of hunger can be deadly in survival mode.

Once you survive your first few nights, the world opens up with endless possibilities: farming, mining for rare ores, exploring strongholds, and even traveling to other dimensions like the Nether or the End.
